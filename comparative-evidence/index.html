Vaccinated versus unvaccinated.

A source-linked review of the most relevant long-term health comparisons, including positive signals, international studies, contested analyses, and large contrary cohorts.

UnprovenClaim that never vaccinating reduces overall chronic illness
Interpretation standard

Observed association ≠ causation. Each study uses different populations, doses, ages, outcomes and follow-up. Study size does not by itself eliminate confounding. Compare raw outcome counts, healthcare visit intensity, verified exposure, negative controls, person-years and confidence intervals before drawing conclusions. This is a curated review, not a registered systematic review.

Funding, institutional influence, and independent reanalysis

A Cochrane synthesis of 60 rotavirus RCTs reported 56 manufacturer-funded or co-funded trials. That creates a legitimate reason to scrutinize comparators, missing data, prespecified outcomes, reporting and independent replication, without automatically invalidating the results. The Vaccine Safety Datalink provides controlled external research access, but not unrestricted raw records. Compensation decisions must be distinguished from demonstrated incidence, as HRSA explains. This collection examines evidence independently of authors' reputations, including records of corrections and retractions.
