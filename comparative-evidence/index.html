Vaccinated versus unvaccinated.

A source-linked review of the most relevant long-term health comparisons, including positive signals, international studies, contested analyses, and large contrary cohorts.

UnprovenClaim that never vaccinating reduces overall chronic illness
Interpretation standard

Observed association ≠ causation. Each study uses different populations, doses, ages, outcomes and follow-up. Study size does not by itself eliminate confounding. Compare raw outcome counts, healthcare visit intensity, verified exposure, negative controls, person-years and confidence intervals before drawing conclusions. This is a curated review, not a registered systematic review.

2000 · JapanPositive association

Yoneyama et al. | DPT and atopic disorders

Small cross-sectional study · 82 preschool children

Reported finding

Asthma: 10/39 DPT-vaccinated (25.6%) vs 1/43 unvaccinated (2.3%).

Critical limitation

Sample is extremely small; non-random vaccine receipt and age/healthcare differences may explain the finding. Older DPT formulation.

2008 · CanadaPositive association

McDonald et al. | Delayed DPT and asthma

Retrospective longitudinal cohort · 11,531 children, all received ≥4 DPT doses

Reported finding

Asthma odds approximately halved among children whose first whole-cell DPT dose was delayed >2 months; multiple delays OR 0.39 (95% CI 0.18–0.86).

Critical limitation

This is timing, not never-vaccinated vs vaccinated. Delays were not randomized; mostly older whole-cell vaccine.

2011 · USAPositive association

DeLong | Vaccination uptake vs autism, states

Ecological regression · U.S. state-level statistics

Reported finding

Higher state-level vaccination uptake correlated with higher recorded autism/speech-language impairment prevalence.

Critical limitation

Ecological fallacy: cannot infer an individual child's vaccine-related risk from state-level correlations; diagnosis, access and reporting confounders.

Mawson et al. | Homeschool survey

Parent-reported cross-sectional convenience sample · 666 homeschool children, 261 unvaccinated

Reported finding

Any chronic condition: 44% vaccinated vs 25% unvaccinated; neurodevelopmental diagnoses 10.5% vs 3.1%.

Critical limitation

Selection bias, parental recall and diagnosis differences, unmatched lifestyle and healthcare utilization.

2020 · USAPositive association

Hooker & Miller | Pediatric EMR comparisons

Retrospective selected-practice cohort · Three U.S. pediatric practices

Reported finding

Vaccination before age 1 associated with developmental-delay OR 2.18; asthma OR 4.49; ear infections OR 2.13.

Critical limitation

Convenience practices, residual confounding and contact-dependent diagnosis ascertainment; not a causal effect.

2021 · USAPositive association

Hooker & Miller | Birth and feeding covariates

Voluntary parent survey, regression · 1,565 primary respondents; 136 fully vaccinated, 945 unvaccinated

Reported finding

Fully vaccinated versus unvaccinated: asthma OR 17.6 (6.94–44.4), autism OR 5.03 (1.64–15.5), ADHD OR 20.8 (4.74–91.2).

Critical limitation

Severe volunteer and group imbalance; fully vaccinated children average about 2 years older; sparse outcome counts; limited covariates.

2025 · USAPositive association

Mawson & Jacob | Florida Medicaid NDDs

Claims-based observational comparison · 47,155 children with nine years enrollment

Reported finding

At least one recorded NDD: 27.8% vaccinated vs 11% classified as unvaccinated; OR 3.12 (2.85–3.41).

Critical limitation

“Unvaccinated” means no vaccination-coded claim, not verified zero doses; vaccine visits proxy healthcare utilization; possible outcome ascertainment bias.

Reported finding

Lower atopy among Steiner children, OR 0.62 (0.43–0.91).

2011 · GermanyNull / contrary

KiGGS | Overall vaccination and health

Representative national cross-sectional survey · 13,453 evaluable, 94 completely unvaccinated

Reported finding

Atopic disease and non-specific infection rates did not show consistent meaningful association with vaccination status; vaccine-preventable disease higher in unvaccinated.

Critical limitation

Fully unvaccinated sample only 94, resulting in wide uncertainty for subgroup outcomes.

Reported finding

Adjusted asthma hazard ratio per +1 mg vaccine aluminum: 1.19 (1.14–1.25) among children without eczema.

2025 · DenmarkNull / contrary

Andersson et al. | Aluminum and 50 conditions

National longitudinal registry cohort · 1,224,176 children

Reported finding

No observed increase across 50 assessed chronic conditions with greater vaccine-aluminum exposure; asthma HR 0.96 (0.94–0.98) per mg.

Critical limitation

Not full-vaccinated vs never-vaccinated causal contrast; questions raised about supplement, residual confounding and positivity.

2026 · InternationalNull / contrary

Doyon-Plourde et al. | Aluminum-adjuvant systematic review

PRISMA systematic review of human safety studies · 59 publications, multiple study designs

Reported finding

No convincing causal association with serious or long-term systemic outcomes. Persistent local nodules/granulomas were consistently supported as uncommon adverse reactions.

Critical limitation

Studies vary in bias and follow-up; evidence is narrower than proving all lifelong schedules harmless. Review addresses aluminum-containing products rather than all vaccines.

Munblit et al. | Audit of Henry Ford unpublished draft

Clinical Infectious Diseases international methods critique · Examines draft cohort of 18,468 children

Reported finding

Identifies exposure misclassification, no aligned time-zero, roughly 7 vs 2 annual healthcare visits, unequal follow-up and multiple outcomes.

Critical limitation

This is critical appraisal, not an independent reanalysis of full patient-level records. Authors disclose vaccine-related consulting and other institutional interests.
